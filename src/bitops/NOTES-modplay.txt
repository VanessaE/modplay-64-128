These are the sources of the Covert Bitops Loadersystem V3.06, by Lasse Oorni
(Cadaver), as released under the MIT license (see README.md, which is also where
the credits are).  Nothing in them has been changed yet, except that the
examples directory, which has binaries in it, isn't here.  They are assembled
with DASM, not ACME, which is how they were written; the Makefiles run dasm on
bitops-check.s, every time, to make sure that they still assemble.  That file is
only a check, and its output isn't used for anything.

The modplayer calls its use of the loader "Bitops".  Any changes that the
modplayer needs to make to these files are made in separate commits, so that
they can be told apart from the originals.

loadexomizer3.s is the Exomizer depacker, which the modplayer doesn't use (yet).
